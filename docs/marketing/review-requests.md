# Review request templates

Review link (from `src/_data/business.json`): https://g.page/r/CdLzueJSP1CMEBM/review

Rules:

- Ask every finished customer, not only the happy ones. Do not pick who gets asked.
- Never offer a gift, discount or payment for a review.
- Never ask for a good review. Ask for an honest one.
- Send by hand. Do not send to people who have not worked with the company.
- Reply to every review in the owner's name, good or bad. Keep replies short and calm.
