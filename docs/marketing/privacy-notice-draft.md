# Privacy notice (draft, not published)

Draft only. The site has no privacy page today. This is plain wording, not legal advice. The owner approves it, and a lawyer should review it before it goes live. Nothing here has been added to the website.

What the code does, as of this branch (`js/analytics.js`, `src/_data/analytics.json`):

- Nothing is sent to Google Analytics until a visitor chooses Accept.
- Decline sends nothing.
- Advertising storage, ad data sharing and ad personalisation stay off. Google signals are off.
- After Accept, the site sends: the page name, the three campaign tags if the page address has them (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign), the name of the site the visitor came from (for example facebook.com, never the page or search words), and these actions: a tap on the phone number, a tap on the email address, the first click into the contact form, and a form send.
- The site never sends form answers, names, phone numbers, emails or addresses to Google Analytics.

## Draft text

**How we use analytics**

We use Google Analytics to learn how people find and use this website. It only runs if you choose Accept in the cookie notice. If you choose Decline, nothing is sent.

If you accept, we share with Google Analytics: which pages you view, which website sent you to us (the site name only), any campaign tag in the link you used, and whether you tapped our phone number or email, started the contact form or sent it. We do not share what you type into the form. We do not use this data for advertising.

You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. The notice will appear again.
