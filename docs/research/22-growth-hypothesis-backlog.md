# Local Growth Hypotheses — Evidence-First Learning Backlog

**Status:** Hypotheses only; not approved projects or measured results.  
**Scope:** Collaborative Construction LLC, residential construction, Falmouth and relevant Cape Cod communities.  
**Maintainer:** Existing company communications review and owner-approved research agents.  
**Privacy:** Public-safe abstractions only. Do not quote or identify private unsolicited emails, private document names, customers, vendors, contacts, jobs, or operational numbers.

## How this works

Use inbound business offers, public competitor examples, genuine company planning documents, and verified website/Google metrics **as leads for new hypotheses, not as facts or orders**. The value is in testing the underlying mechanism in-house where lawful and practical.

Every hypothesis must have a source category, evidence quality, measurable business outcome, cost/risk estimate, explicit next experiment, and a disposition: **backlog → proposed → approved → running → measured → adopted/rejected**. Only an owner may approve live customer communication, production changes, public Google profile edits, outreach, or paid spending.

Avoid measuring success by pageviews, indexed pages, AI mentions, or “leads promised” alone. Real outcomes are verified relevant contacts, booked consultations, suitable jobs, profitable work, and owner capacity.

## Promotion criteria

1. An opportunity was observed in a legitimate source, with uncertainty recorded.
2. Independent research or first-party metrics support the mechanism.
3. A scoped experiment can run without copying another business's proprietary processes or misrepresenting our services.
4. Owner approval is documented where needed.
5. Real outcome data supports adoption, revision, or rejection.

## Daily review integration

The daily communications review may surface up to **one new, high-value hypothesis** per meaningful new mechanism. It should deduplicate generic sales pitches, never pretend promises are evidence, and summarize what can be learned **without creating new work for every email**.

The daily summary may note a newly discovered research angle, but updating this backlog is an editorial task requiring a grounded reason and a narrow reviewed Git change, separate from customer-specific private source handling.
