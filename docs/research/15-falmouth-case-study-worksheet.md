## Explicit guardrails for whoever completes this

- Do not publish a case study, update the gallery caption, or add a testimonial based on assumptions from the photos alone.
- If the project turns out to be prior personal/employment work rather than LLC work, it cannot be presented as "our work" — it would need either honest framing (e.g., "built by [person] prior to forming this LLC") or exclusion from company marketing entirely.
- If photo or testimonial permission cannot be confirmed, the photos may need to stay in the gallery only as already-published (unchanged), without added public attribution, quotes, or expanded claims — or be reconsidered for removal if permission was never actually granted in the first place.
- Once this worksheet is filled in and approved, the next step is a separate, explicit decision to draft public-facing case-study copy — not a default output of filling in this form.
