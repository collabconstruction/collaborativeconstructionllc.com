## Notes on coverage

The monitor's 26 entries encompass 16 of the owner's originally specified 18 plus added specialty/design references, and the two latest entries. This audit opened each configured homepage once, with one retrieval failure. It did **not** establish who is running paid advertisements. Public testimonials on company websites are self-selected material, not independently verified customer satisfaction. Most pages contain no reliable same-scope price. Mark price, recent ads, public Google review count/rating, date of last social post and mobile quality as **UNKNOWN** for all until separately checked using first-party listings or measured tests. Do not say "they run no ads" or "they have no reviews."

## Gap analysis relevant to Collaborative Construction

1. **Own the difference between building and independent homeowner oversight.** Duffany, McPhee and PSD also describe project management, but these firms often manage their own builds. Proposed differentiation: a clear "already have a builder? independent oversight" path. Do not claim no competitors offer it. Label: estimate, strategic interpretation of public descriptions.
2. **Publish a concise three-stage process and a scope/discovery checklist.** Longfellow, Barnes, REEF and Tigo explain journey and risk better than a bare service listing. Create wholly original homeowner FAQ on permits, change orders, communication and scheduling.
3. **Build truthful work examples, not imaginary testimonials.** Photograph provenance, owner approval, date/town, role on project, problem and outcome. Avoid fabricated projects or AI-made images presented as completed work.
4. **Improve the lead form's trust.** Show written scope expectations, typical first-contact steps and registration where verified. Explain that permit feasibility and costs depend on lot and town.
5. **Segment competitors.** Luxury custom builders (Cataldo, McPhee, PSD) differ from direct general remodelers (Cape Cod Remodeling, High Tide). Specialty crews (Care Free Homes, Fuccillo) are supplier/referral candidates; do not treat all 26 as interchangeable.
