## Immediate interpretation

The evidence does not support “nothing is happening.” Housing policy, wastewater construction, ongoing permits and an established renovation market provide concrete activity to monitor. It also does not show that Collaborative Construction currently has enough visibility, sales follow-through, proof, capacity or pricing fit to capture that activity. Those are separate questions that require company lead and job data.

Prioritize Falmouth and genuinely serviceable nearby towns. A geographically broad website creates little value if travel time, crew availability, ferry logistics or subcontractor coverage prevent profitable delivery. Test one or two service/geography combinations first, such as Falmouth renovation planning and Upper Cape ADU feasibility, then expand based on booked gross profit and delivery capacity.

## Offers to test, subject to actual capability

### 1. Renovation planning consultation

Deliver a written scope outline, known unknowns, next professional inputs and a decision sequence. Explain whether this is a free introductory call or a paid site assessment before the customer books. Do not use a general marketing consultation as a substitute for design or engineering work.

Pilot: ten qualified inquiries or eight weeks, whichever yields a useful sample. Track consultation bookings, attendance, time invested, estimate requests, booked jobs, expected gross profit and reasons the project did not progress. Success is worthwhile projects with manageable estimating time.

### 2. ADU feasibility pathway

Intake: town, relationship to existing building, approximate area, intended use, existing plans, utilities, site access, timeframe and budget readiness. Store exact property details in access-controlled project records. The public knowledge base should contain process and aggregate findings.

Deliverable: a dated checklist of questions for the owner and qualified local professionals, not an automatic permit-eligibility verdict. Separate state definitions, local implementation, wastewater, conservation/flood constraints, building-code review and project economics. Record what remains unverified.

Pilot: one professionally reviewed information page and one intake route; no paid traffic until tracking works and the owner confirms capacity. Measure qualified requests, site reviews, specialist handoffs and viable proposals. Record the top reasons a project is infeasible or deferred; those findings improve the offer more than raw page views.

### 3. Remote-owner renovation communication

This is a customer hypothesis supported by competitors' advertised seasonal-home services, not a quantified company demand forecast. [Cape Associates' Falmouth offer](https://www.capeassociates.com/falmouth-ma), [Custom Crafted's property-care service](https://www.customcraftedcc.com/).

Offer a clear decision calendar, approved progress photos, selection deadlines and weekly status reports on projects the company actually accepts. Test the value through homeowner interviews and inquiry attribution. Avoid expanding into ongoing property management until staffing, insurance, responsibilities and margins have been assessed.

## Limits and unresolved questions

- Local permit records and the ADU tracker have been identified but no town-level counts were extracted in this pass. No Falmouth ADU market-size estimate should be inferred from statewide totals.
- The direct state ADU hub returned an intermittent access error after search retrieval. Reopen and verify its current documents before quoting a rule or publishing homeowner guidance. A separate state policy page returned conflicting size language in search; this review relies on the explicit FAQ and leaves current implementation for verification.
- No specific open public bid was validated as a fit. The Great Harbor ramp notice found during discovery closed January 14, 2026, so it must not be presented as a current lead. [Historical notice](https://www.falmouthma.gov/DocumentCenter/View/20626/Invitation-for-Bids---Great-Harbor-Ramp-Reconstruction?bidId=)
- Existing Google Business Profile statistics, Search Console impressions, customer inquiries, lead-to-contract conversion, project margins, licenses and team capacity were not available to this research task.
- This is a Falmouth/Upper Cape starting point. Islands and South Shore research should be scoped separately once actual service coverage is confirmed.
