## Owner-side management pricing models

Published industry example: [Mastt, Dec 3 2025](https://www.mastt.com/blogs/owners-representative-construction-fee) says 1%–5% of project cost or $75–$200+ per hour; another commercial-project oriented provider gives different figures [Terrapin 2026](https://terrapincg.com/news/owners-representative-cost-2026). These are broad industry self-published references, **not quotes from Massachusetts residential firms**, and must not become a public rate card. Options for later owner decision: hourly advisory with cap, fixed-fee milestone review, project percentage with scope/exclusions, monthly retainer. Document independence, conflict-of-interest risk, reporting frequency, liability and written service scope.

## What drives genuine cost variation

Site access, existing conditions, sewer/septic upgrades, utilities, foundation, flood/coastal zone, permitting, material lead times, island transport, labor scheduling, demolition hazards, drawings/engineering, finish selections, occupied-site protection and change orders. For Massachusetts ADUs, the statewide by-right framework does not eliminate site-specific building-code and permitting constraints: [MA official ADU FAQ, updated Oct 14 2026](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/accessory-dwelling-unit-adu-faqs).

## Price claim guardrails

"Affordable and competitive" is **owner-confirmation-needed** because no like-for-like quotes, actual contribution margins or approved service scope were provided. Massachusetts HIC rules forbid misleading advertising and require current registration number for applicable contractor advertising: [official HIC resources](https://www.mass.gov/info-details/hic-contractor-resources). Safer draft for legal/owner review: "We work with you to define scope, priorities and a clear proposal before work begins." This does not claim to be cheap or cheaper. Do not invent estimates, years or price savings.

## Questions to establish a price policy

Owner privately decides accepted work types, minimum/maximum job size, direct cost and overhead allocation, markup, risk contingency, target contribution margin, deposit schedule compliant with applicable law, travel and island premiums, change-order approval, whether owner's oversight is separate from GC services. Price examples should only be published after verified historical anonymized jobs and owner approval.
