THE SHORTLIST

B for balance. A for refinement. E for distinction.

My choice: B / Cape, Considered. Its warmth fits renovation and residential inquiries without forcing a luxury-only identity. Pair it with a genuinely distinctive joined mark and real project/process evidence. Green alone is not distinctive.

A / Measured Architecture is the premium alternative. E / Shared Framework offers the strongest recognition potential and the greatest interpretation risk. C is the lowest-effort visual evolution from the current charcoal system; D is the quiet craftsmanship alternative.
