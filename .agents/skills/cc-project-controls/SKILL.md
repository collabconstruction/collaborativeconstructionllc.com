# Construction project controls

Use only the assigned project's authorized drawings, schedule, logs and cost summaries. Read the private project-control template in `docs/research/12-templates.md`. Identify reporting cutoff and revision/date for each source; do not combine superseded drawings with current quantities silently.

Separate observed site facts, reported progress and assumptions. Separate proposed, approved, executed and billed changes. For each exception name source, responsible person, due date, dependency and decision required. Verify units, scale and quantity provenance before calculations; missing scale is a blocker for takeoff, not permission to assume 1:100.

Produce a two-week look-ahead, overdue client decisions/RFIs, procurement risks, pending changes and estimate-versus-actual exceptions. Do not treat AI drawing extraction as validated measurements or make engineering/safety/code compliance determinations. The appropriate professional confirms site conditions, quantities and commitments.

Protect client identities and job finances. Public case-study extracts require approved role/scope and publication permissions; anonymization alone may not sufficiently protect a recognizable project. Capture lessons that improve future estimates and sales promises without exposing private records.

Deliver the exceptions and next decisions, not an unsupported assertion that the project is on time or on budget. See `05-github-skills.md` before considering any third-party takeoff tool; referenced tools are not installed by this skill.
