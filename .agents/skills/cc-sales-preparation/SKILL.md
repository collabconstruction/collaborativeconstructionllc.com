# Sales preparation

Read approved services/geography and the owner's decisions in the company review and decision log. Use only assigned customer context through authorized access. Keep identifiable lead records in the private sales system; public Markdown receives sanitized process notes or aggregates.

Qualify town, service, scope, timing, budget readiness, decision-maker needs and fit with actual capacity. Missing budget or plans is an information gap, not automatic rejection. Record next action and owner. Distinguish an introductory conversation, site visit, budget indication, detailed estimate and paid preconstruction; don't change the advertised free-estimate promise by assumption.

For proposal preparation list scope, exclusions, allowances, dependencies, drawing/revision references, open decisions and dated source quotes. Do not invent quantities, unit prices, schedules, credentials or binding terms. Identify the company's builder versus independent representative role and potential conflicts.

Return a concise intake summary, missing questions, a human-editable follow-up draft and proposal outline if requested. Sending, pricing commitments and contracts require the user's applicable authorization and professional review. No automatic customer messages are created by this skill.

Use the lead and project templates in `12-templates.md`; route booked outcomes into the scorecard with the proper cohort and job ID.
