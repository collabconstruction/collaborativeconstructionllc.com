# Company intelligence

Find the project root through `AGENTS.md`; read `docs/research/00-INDEX.md`, `01-company-review.md` and `11-decisions-and-questions.md`. For a company review, inspect relevant `src/_data/` records and current source rather than treating prior prose as authoritative.

Separate observed source claims, owner-verified facts, hypotheses and unknowns. Keep personal career experience distinct from LLC history. Current GA4 configuration supersedes historical installation assumptions but does not prove received data. Do not use the September baseline as today's lead count or performance.

Reconcile conflicts into the decision log with both sources and dates. Preserve history and mark superseded claims. For growth planning, use qualified customers, job contribution, available starts and owner workload; request missing inputs without inventing targets.

When coordinating agents, use `09-agent-pipeline.md` and `12-templates.md`: exact output ownership, bounded input access, evidence reviewer, measurable acceptance. Deliver an updated fact/decision record, prioritized next actions and explicit blockers. Do not claim Claude reviewed anything without a successful artifact.
