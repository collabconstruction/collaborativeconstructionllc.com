# Local construction growth

Read the company index, confirmed service/geography decisions, `03-competitors.md`, `04-local-opportunities.md` and `07-website-and-profile.md` as relevant. Falmouth/Upper Cape is an initial hypothesis, not permission to assume every Cape/South Shore/Islands job is viable.

For each market claim capture direct source, observation date, event/effective date, current status and company relevance. Prefer municipal/state sources for permitting and infrastructure. Distinguish proposed policy, adopted policy, application, issued permit and awarded work. Public permit activity is not proof of an available customer.

Competitor websites establish self-reported positioning, not independent quality, revenue or market share. Do not infer Google Maps rank from search-result order or invent review counts. Expand the discovery set only where the actual target market requires it.

Recommend one or two experiments with audience, proof required, effort, qualified-lead outcome and guardrail. Use authentic projects and specific process evidence; no cloned town pages or unverified project claims. Draft materials within authorization, but don't contact prospects, post, edit GBP or spend without applicable user instruction.

Output a sourced memo using `12-templates.md`, with limitations, refresh trigger and exact next decision. Route measurement to `06-data-access.md` and `10-scorecard.md`.
