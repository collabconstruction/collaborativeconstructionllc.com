Service Area Two

Clerk of the Works

On-site supervision by our Clerk of the Works throughout every building activity is one of the most effective tools for cost containment, safeguarding quality control and keeping the work true to the plans and specifications. Across each stage of construction, we provide:

  • ✓ Project-records maintenance, including contractor certificates of insurance
  • ✓ Process & quality control
  • ✓ Review of the general contractor's record-keeping
  • ✓ Monitoring the construction schedule & identifying potential delays
  • ✓ Construction-cost monitoring
  • ✓ Safety-program review
  • ✓ Investigation of & recommendations on proposed change orders
  • ✓ Assistance negotiating the cost of proposed change orders
  • ✓ Review of contractor progress-payment requests
  • ✓ Shop-drawing & submittal review (samples and other submittals)
  • ✓ Monthly progress-report submission
Service Area Three

Construction Advisory

We don't manage the job site. We protect the investment. While the Owner's Representative drives day-to-day project execution and the Lender monitors budget compliance, when we act as a Construction Advisor, we provide high-level risk management and specialized technical foresight. Think of us as the strategic advisor for the project, ensuring both alignment and protection across all stakeholders.

Your Owner's Rep is in the trenches every day making sure contractor submittals are in and the schedule moves forward. We serve as your executive sounding board: reviewing high-stakes contract terms, auditing schedule logic before critical delays happen, and giving you objective, peer-level guidance when tough decisions arise.
